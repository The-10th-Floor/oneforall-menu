- **[HIGH] ⚑ Counterfeit NTAG213/215/216 chips circulate on marketplaces; genuine NXP chips carry an ECDSA originality signature that the NXP TagInfo app (Android/iOS) can check, so test a sample before bulk-buying.**  
  GoToTags: NXP's HSM signs each chip's 7-byte UID (ECDSA, 224-bit curve, 56-byte signature at page 0x2C on NTAG21x); 'counterfeit NFC chips exist on the market, often masquerading as' NTAG models and are exposed by an invalid signature. NXP's TagInfo page confirms the free app on Google Play and the App Store identifies IC type and manufacturer and dumps memory; rfidtagworld states its security analysis 'can also detect counterfeit or cloned tags'. Practical selection rule for AliExpress: title must say NXP NTAG213/215 (not 'compatible' or 'FM' chips), 1,000+ sold and 4.8+ rating, order a 10-pack first, scan with TagInfo and confirm 'NXP NTAG215' plus originality check OK before ordering 100. For a plain menu URL, NTAG213 (144 bytes user memory, URLs up to about 136 characters per Seritag) is sufficient; use NTAG215 if the URL is long or you may add a vCard later.  
  ↳ https://gototags.com/help/nfc/chip/features/originality-signature

## B · Fabrication — 3D printing & laser acrylic (Amman/Irbid)

Host: Cloudflare Pages is the clear pick — static requests are "free and unlimited" with no bandwidth charge, 500 builds/month, automatic SSL on custom domains, and Cloudflare runs an Amman (AMM) edge PoP, whereas GitHub Pages rides Fastly (nearest PoPs Dubai/Fujairah/Riyadh, no Amman) and its ToS bars "running your online business", and Vercel Hobby is explicitly non-commercial; Netlify Free allows commercial use but hard-pauses the site ("Site not available") when its 300 monthly credits run out. Link layer: Short.io Free (1,000 links, 5 custom domains, 50,000 tracked clicks/month, destination editing on every plan, CNAME to cname.short.io) beats Bitly Free (5 links/month, no analytics, no custom domain, destination locked), Dub (pricing page now starts at $90/month; its help doc still claims a free tier — contradictory), and Cloudflare Redirect Rules (10 free per zone but no hit counts). Domain: the official Jordanian registry (MoDEE, dns.jo) charges 25 JOD/yr for .com.jo and 50 JOD/yr for .jo, paid via eFAWATEERcom, with a commercial-registration certificate as the company document — foreign registrars quote $290–455/yr plus a notarized POA, so register direct; .com is $11.08 and .menu ~$27 at Porkbun as fallbacks. Design references verified vendor-direct: TAPro's 2.75" mirror-gloss black acrylic NFC menu disc ($27), Dang Designs' layered-acrylic + gold/rose-gold mirrored-badge NFC sign (CAD $150, custom shapes), PhoneTapify's edge-lit LED NFC lightbox ($207.90), plus Taggytech/TAPiTAG/Ctrl-Alt-Engrave/Laser Arts; premium tag components exist off the shelf (Shop NFC gold-reflective epoxy-dome on-metal NTAG tags €1.83 MOQ 100; battery-free LED NFC tags that flash on tap $0.30–0.80 MOQ 100). ## C · NFC technical + UX spec

For a single https menu link, the right chip is a genuine NXP NTAG213 (144 B user memory, ~136-char URL, NFC Forum Type 2, 10-yr retention, 32-bit password, lockable): NXP's page and Seritag's guidance both say bigger NTAG215/216 add nothing to scan performance and Seritag "strongly recommend[s] NTAG213" for URL tags; NTAG 424 DNA (416 B, AES-128 SUN, ~€0.5-1.1) is only for anti-counterfeit, and ICODE SLIX (ISO 15693/Type 5) has a documented iPhone background-reading failure mode, so avoid it. Antenna size matters more than chip: Seritag's data puts a 38 mm tag with a 34 mm antenna at 5-6 cm on a good Android, "anything over 30 mm" gives a good phone experience, and discs under 25 mm should be avoided. Acrylic, PLA/PETG, glass and ceramic tile are transparent to 13.56 MHz (3 mm printed acrylic NFC panels are a stock product; 3D-print guides say 1-2 mm of wall over the tag reads best), but metal kills the field: a standard tag needs ≥3 mm stand-off from metal to match an on-metal tag and ≥6 mm to beat it, so keep the tag off the teal window frames if they are metal or use a ferrite-backed IP68 NTAG213 disc. Apple's own doc confirms iPhone XS and later read NDEF URI records in the background with no app (opens Safari) but not when locked-since-boot, in Airplane mode, with the camera open or Wallet active; iPhone 7/8/X need the Control Center "NFC Tag Reader"; iPhone antennas sit top-back and background scanning is weaker than in-app, so design for that worst case.
